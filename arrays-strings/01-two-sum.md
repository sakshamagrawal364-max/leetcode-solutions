## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach
I used two nested loops to check every pair of numbers.
When the sum of two numbers equals the target, their indices are returned.

### Complexity
- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes
The solution was tested locally with a normal case and a duplicate-values case before submitting to LeetCode.
