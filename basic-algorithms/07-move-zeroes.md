## Problem: Move Zeroes (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/move-zeroes/

### Approach
I moved all non-zero elements to the beginning of the array while maintaining their original order.
Then I filled the remaining positions with zeroes.

### Complexity
- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes
The solution was tested locally with a normal case and a single-zero edge case before submitting to LeetCode.
